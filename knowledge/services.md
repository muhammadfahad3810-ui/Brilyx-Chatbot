# Brilyx Services

Brilyx offers four core services. This document is the source of truth for
what each service is, what it does, and who it is for. The AI assistant must
not claim capabilities, integrations, or features beyond what is written
here.

---

## 1. AI Website Chatbots

### Purpose

Give a business website an AI assistant that can talk to visitors, answer
questions, and help guide them toward the right next step.

### Core Capabilities

- Answering visitor questions about the business, in natural language
- Explaining services, general pricing, and how things work
- Helping qualify visitor needs by understanding their problem
- Guiding visitors toward a next step (e.g. a demo or human contact)

### Typical Use Cases

- A visitor lands on a website and has questions before contacting the business
- A business wants to reduce repetitive questions handled manually
- A business wants a friendlier, faster first response than a static contact form

### Ideal Customers

- Businesses with a website that gets visitor traffic but limited staff to
  respond instantly
- Businesses that want a more interactive alternative to static FAQ pages

### Example Business Problems Solved

- Visitors leave the site without getting answers, resulting in lost inquiries
- Staff spend time answering the same repetitive questions
- No way to engage visitors outside business hours

## 2. WhatsApp AI Agents

### Purpose

Let a business communicate with customers on WhatsApp through an AI agent
that can hold conversations, answer questions, and support the customer
journey.

### Core Capabilities

- Responding to customer messages on WhatsApp in natural language
- Answering questions about the business, services, and general pricing
- Helping guide conversations toward useful outcomes (e.g. booking, inquiry,
  handoff to a human)

### Typical Use Cases

- A business receives customer inquiries on WhatsApp and wants faster,
  consistent responses
- A business wants to handle common questions automatically while keeping a
  human available for complex cases

### Ideal Customers

- Businesses that already use WhatsApp as a primary customer communication
  channel
- Businesses with customer volumes that make manual WhatsApp replies slow or
  inconsistent

### Example Business Problems Solved

- Slow response times on WhatsApp lead to lost customers
- Staff are overloaded answering repetitive WhatsApp questions
- Inconsistent answers are given by different staff members

## 3. Business Automation

### Purpose

Automate repetitive, manual business processes so staff can focus on
higher-value work.

### Core Capabilities

- Identifying repetitive manual tasks and workflows
- Automating steps in business processes
- Connecting different parts of a business workflow together

### Typical Use Cases

- A business repeats the same manual steps for every customer or order
- A business wants fewer manual handoffs between tools or people

### Ideal Customers

- Businesses with clear, repeatable manual processes
- Businesses looking to reduce operational overhead

### Example Business Problems Solved

- Time lost on manual, repetitive administrative work
- Errors introduced by manual data entry or manual handoffs
- Slow internal processes that delay customer-facing outcomes

---

## 4. Websites & Software

### Purpose

Design and build websites and custom software tailored to a business's
needs.

### Core Capabilities

- Building business websites
- Building custom software tailored to specific business requirements

### Typical Use Cases

- A business needs a professional website
- A business needs custom software to support its operations

### Ideal Customers

- Businesses without an existing website or with an outdated one
- Businesses with needs that off-the-shelf software doesn't fully cover

### Example Business Problems Solved

- No professional online presence
- Existing tools don't fit the business's actual workflow

## Integration Policy

Brilyx does not claim support for any specific third-party platform,
software, or integration unless it has been explicitly confirmed. If a
visitor asks whether a specific tool or platform is supported, the assistant
must say this needs to be confirmed with the Brilyx team rather than
guessing or assuming compatibility.
