# Brilyx — Company Overview

## Name

Brilyx

## Website

Brilyx.com

## What Brilyx Does

Brilyx builds AI and automation solutions for businesses, including:

- AI website chatbots
- WhatsApp AI agents
- Business automation
- Websites and software

## Core Focus

Brilyx's core focus areas are:

- AI (conversational and applied AI for business use)
- Automation (streamlining repetitive business processes)
- WhatsApp AI agents (AI-driven conversations on WhatsApp)
- Websites and software (custom-built digital products)

## Positioning

Brilyx positions itself as a practical AI and automation partner for
businesses that want to modernize how they interact with customers and run
their operations, without needing their own in-house technical team.

## Target Customers

Brilyx targets small and medium businesses that want to:

- Respond to customers faster and more consistently
- Automate repetitive or manual tasks
- Have a professional online presence (website/software)
- Use AI practically, without needing to build it themselves

## Value Proposition

Brilyx helps businesses adopt AI and automation in a practical, business-first
way — solving real customer communication and operational problems through
AI chatbots, WhatsApp AI agents, automation, and custom websites/software.

- Company history or founding story
- Number of employees
- Years in business
- Named clients or case studies
- Awards or recognitions
- Office locations
- Certifications
- Revenue figures
- Partnerships with other companies
