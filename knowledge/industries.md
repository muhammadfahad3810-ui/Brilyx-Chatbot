# Brilyx Industries

Brilyx's services are general-purpose, but some industries have common,
well-understood needs that map naturally to what Brilyx offers. This
document describes typical problems and opportunities in those industries —
it does not claim that Brilyx currently has customers in them.

---

## Primary Industries

### Dental Clinics

**Common Problems**

- Missed calls and delayed responses to patient inquiries
- Repetitive questions about services, hours, and appointment availability
- Manual, time-consuming appointment scheduling and reminders

**Potential Automation Opportunities**

- Answering common patient questions automatically via chatbot or WhatsApp
- Automating appointment reminders and follow-ups
- Streamlining new-patient inquiry handling

**Relevant Brilyx Services**

- AI Website Chatbots
- WhatsApp AI Agents
- Business Automation

---

### Medical Clinics

**Common Problems**

- High volume of routine patient questions (hours, services, availability)
- Front-desk staff overloaded with repetitive communication
- Inconsistent information given to patients across channels

**Potential Automation Opportunities**

- AI-assisted first response for common patient questions
- Automated appointment-related communication
- Consistent, always-available answers across website and WhatsApp

**Relevant Brilyx Services**

- AI Website Chatbots
- WhatsApp AI Agents
- Business Automation

---

### Healthcare Businesses (General)

**Common Problems**

- Difficulty scaling patient/customer communication as demand grows
- Manual coordination between front-desk, scheduling, and follow-up
- Limited after-hours responsiveness

**Potential Automation Opportunities**

- AI-driven first-line responses on website and WhatsApp
- Automating repetitive administrative workflows
- Improving after-hours engagement without adding staff

**Relevant Brilyx Services**

- AI Website Chatbots
- WhatsApp AI Agents
- Business Automation
- Websites & Software

---

### Restaurants

**Common Problems**

- Repeated questions about menu, hours, location, and reservations
- Manual handling of reservation or order-related inquiries
- Inconsistent responsiveness during busy periods

**Potential Automation Opportunities**

- Chatbot/WhatsApp AI agent for menu, hours, and reservation-related
  questions
- Automating repetitive customer communication during peak times

**Relevant Brilyx Services**

- AI Website Chatbots
- WhatsApp AI Agents
- Websites & Software

---

### Cafes

**Common Problems**

- Frequent repetitive questions about menu, hours, and location
- Limited staff availability to handle inquiries during busy hours
- No easy way for customers to get quick answers outside opening hours

**Potential Automation Opportunities**

- AI chatbot/WhatsApp agent for common customer questions
- Basic automation of repetitive customer communication

**Relevant Brilyx Services**

- AI Website Chatbots
- WhatsApp AI Agents
- Websites & Software

---

## Secondary Industries

Brilyx's services are not limited to the primary industries above. Any
business that needs AI, automation, a website, or custom software is a
potential fit — the specific problems and opportunities should be assessed
based on that business's actual situation rather than assumed from industry
alone.

## Important Note

Brilyx does not currently claim existing customers in any of the industries
listed above. These are descriptions of common problems and how Brilyx's
services could address them, not case studies or client claims.
