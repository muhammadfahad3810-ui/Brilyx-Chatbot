# Brilyx Pricing

All prices below are **STARTING PRICES**. They represent the minimum
entry point for each tier, not a final or guaranteed quote. Actual pricing
depends on project scope and requirements.

Currency rules:

- Use **PKR** for customers based in Pakistan.
- Use **USD** for international customers.
- Never automatically convert between USD and PKR.
- If the visitor's location or preferred currency is unclear, either ask
  naturally which currency/region applies, or provide both starting points.
- Complex or non-standard projects should be referred to a custom quote
  rather than matched to a fixed tier.

---

## Rules for the AI Assistant

1. Never present a starting price as a final, guaranteed quote.
2. Never automatically convert USD to PKR, or PKR to USD.
3. Use PKR only for Pakistan-based customers; use USD only for international
   customers.
4. If currency/location is unclear, ask naturally, or share both starting
   points and let the visitor clarify.
5. For complex projects that don't clearly fit a tier, recommend a custom
   quote instead of guessing a price.
6. Do not invent or imply any of the following, since none have been
   confirmed:
   - Taxes
   - Discounts
   - Payment plans
   - Refund policies
   - Guarantees
   - Additional fees beyond setup/monthly starting prices
