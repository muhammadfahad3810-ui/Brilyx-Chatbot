# Brilyx FAQ

These FAQ entries are based only on confirmed information in this knowledge
base. Where information is not yet confirmed, the answer explicitly says so
and refers the question to the Brilyx team, rather than guessing.

---

**Q: What is Brilyx?**

A: Brilyx is a company that builds AI and automation solutions for
businesses — including AI website chatbots, WhatsApp AI agents, business
automation, and websites/software. See `company.md`.

---

**Q: What services does Brilyx provide?**

A: Brilyx provides four core services: AI Website Chatbots, WhatsApp AI
Agents, Business Automation, and Websites & Software. See `services.md` for
details on each.

---

**Q: What is an AI chatbot?**

A: In the context of Brilyx's services, an AI website chatbot is an AI
assistant added to a business's website that can answer visitor questions,
explain services and general pricing, and help guide visitors to a next
step like requesting a demo.

---

**Q: What is a WhatsApp AI agent?**

A: A WhatsApp AI agent is an AI assistant that communicates with customers
over WhatsApp — answering questions and supporting the customer journey,
similar to an AI website chatbot but on WhatsApp.

---

**Q: What is business automation?**

A: Business automation, as offered by Brilyx, means identifying repetitive
manual business processes and automating them, so staff spend less time on
repetitive tasks and fewer errors occur from manual handoffs.

---

**Q: How much does a chatbot start at?**

A: Starting prices depend on the tier and region. For Pakistan, pricing
starts at PKR 18,000+ setup and PKR 3,000+/month (Starter tier). For
international customers, pricing starts at $100+ setup and $20+/month
(Starter tier). These are starting prices, not final quotes — see
`pricing.md`.

---

**Q: Do Pakistan and international customers have different pricing?**

A: Yes. Brilyx uses PKR pricing for Pakistan-based customers and USD pricing
for international customers. These are not automatically converted between
each other. See `pricing.md`.

---

**Q: Can Brilyx build custom software?**

A: Yes, custom software is part of the Websites & Software service. Specific
scope and pricing depend on the requirements, and complex projects are
handled with a custom quote.

---

**Q: Can Brilyx build solutions for other industries?**

A: Yes. While dental clinics, medical clinics, healthcare businesses,
restaurants, and cafes are primary industries of focus, Brilyx's services
apply to any business that needs AI, automation, a website, or software. See
`industries.md`.

---

**Q: Can I request a demo?**

A: Yes, visitors can ask for a free demo. Note: a demo is only actually
created, sent, or escalated to the team once the backend process for that
exists — the assistant should not claim a demo has been created, sent, or
that a team member has been notified unless that action has actually
happened. See `demo.md`.

---

**Q: Can I speak to a human?**

A: Yes, the assistant can offer to hand off to a member of the Brilyx team
when appropriate, especially for complex requests or when the visitor asks
directly.

---

**Q: What happens if my requirements are complex?**

A: Complex or non-standard projects are best handled with a custom quote
rather than a fixed pricing tier. The assistant should recommend a custom
quote and, where appropriate, offer to connect the visitor with the Brilyx
team.

---

**Q: I have a question that isn't covered here — what happens?**

A: If the answer isn't confirmed in this knowledge base, the assistant
should say the information isn't available yet and offer to refer the
question to the Brilyx team, rather than guessing or inventing an answer.
