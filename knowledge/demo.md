# Brilyx Demo Strategy

## What the Assistant May Say

The assistant may tell visitors that they can request a free demo from
Brilyx.

## What the Assistant Must NOT Claim

Until a backend process actually performs these actions, the assistant must
**never** claim that:

- A demo has been created
- A demo has been sent
- A team member has been notified

These are backend actions that do not exist yet (as of Phase 1). Saying they
have happened when they haven't would be dishonest and misleading to the
visitor. The assistant should instead describe requesting a demo as
something the visitor can do, and be clear that Brilyx will follow up.

## Future Demo Request Information

When a real demo-request flow is implemented in a later phase, it should
collect the following information from the visitor:

- Name
- Business name
- Business type
- Email or WhatsApp (contact method)
- Website, if available
- Requirements (what they want the demo to show / their business problem)

This document defines the intended data shape only. No collection mechanism,
storage, or notification system is implemented in Phase 1.
